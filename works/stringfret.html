弦外之音 · Overtones

六根弦，一套可以调的物理。声音由数字波导实时计算，没有采样、没有循环：按品改变延迟线长度决定音高，弦撞到品丝被非线性折返，就是打品。

Six strings, one editable physics. Every note is solved in real time by a digital waveguide — no samples, no loops. Fretting sets the delay length (pitch); the string hitting a fret is folded back non-linearly, which is the buzz.

自动演奏：右上「自动演奏」会让琴按当前参数自己弹一段循环，点右上「停止」结束。它只是试听，不是录制。

Auto play: the AUTO PLAY button lets the instrument play a generated loop with the current settings; press STOP to end it. Nothing is recorded.

怎么玩：点弦发声；拖参数下的轨道改数值；品距拖到 0.2 以下会持续打品；手指位置会切到滑音模式。

How to play: click a string to pluck; drag a parameter track to change it; action below 0.2 buzzes continuously; finger position switches to slide mode.
